# Project Overview

## 📖 Introduction
The **Student Engagement Analysis** project aims to explore and analyze the engagement patterns of students based on their subscription plans over two years (Q4 2021 to Q4 2022). The key focus is to examine changes in engagement levels among low-engagement students (who watched between 1 and 100 minutes in 2021) and determine how different subscription plans impact their learning behavior.

## 🎯 Objectives
- Analyze engagement trends for **Paid** and **Free** plan students.
- Calculate **descriptive statistics** to summarize engagement levels.
- Examine **skewness and kurtosis** to understand distribution patterns.
- Compute **confidence intervals** to estimate student engagement levels.
- Perform **hypothesis testing** to compare engagement between Paid and Free plan students.

## 🔍 Key Insights
- Paid-plan students showed **higher engagement growth** from Q4 2021 to Q4 2022.
- Free-plan students had **more variation** in their engagement, with some watching significantly more content while others disengaged.
- Statistical tests confirmed **significant differences** in engagement between Paid and Free plan students.

This project provides valuable insights into how different subscription models impact student learning behavior and engagement levels.
