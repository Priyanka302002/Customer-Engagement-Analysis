# Data Description

## Dataset Overview
The dataset provides information on student engagement across two years (Q4 2021 to Q4 2022) based on their subscription plans. The data is structured to analyze engagement patterns and statistical insights for students on free and paid plans.

## Columns in the Dataset
- **Student ID**: A unique identifier assigned to each student.
- **Plan Type**: The subscription type, categorized as either "Paid" or "Free".
- **Q4 2021 Minutes Watched**: The total number of minutes watched by the student during Q4 2021.
- **Q4 2022 Minutes Watched**: The total number of minutes watched by the student during Q4 2022.

## Dataset Structure
The dataset is divided into multiple sheets for different analyses:
1. **Descriptive Statistics**: Summary statistics (mean, median, standard deviation) of student engagement for both free and paid plans.
2. **Skewness and Kurtosis**: Measures the asymmetry and tail distribution of engagement data.
3. **Confidence Intervals**: Estimates the range within which the true mean minutes watched lies for each group.
4. **Hypothesis Testing**: Statistical tests comparing engagement between paid and free-plan students.

## Data Characteristics
- **Paid-plan students**: Tend to show higher engagement levels, especially in Q4 2022.
- **Free-plan students**: Engagement levels vary widely, with some students showing minimal activity while others watch significantly more content.
- **Engagement Trends**: Changes between Q4 2021 and Q4 2022 help identify the effectiveness of different subscription plans.

This structured dataset serves as the foundation for further analysis and insights into student engagement trends.
